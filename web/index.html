  1. Choose two languages.

    Tell us what you speak and what you'd like to hear.

  2. Speak, or write.

    Allow microphone access, then record up to 30 seconds. Pause to finish, or tap the button again.

  3. Make yourself understood.

    Read, listen, copy, or download your translation. Edit the transcript whenever you need to.

Your recording goes to Google for transcription. Text is translated by Google or MyMemory, then sent to ElevenLabs for speech. Session history stays in this browser tab and clears when you reload. A key entered here stays in this tab until cleared or reloaded and is sent through the JT World server to ElevenLabs only for audio. It is not saved to disk. The public website requires your own key for audio; text translation does not require one. If your selected voice requires a paid plan, a premade multilingual voice is used.
